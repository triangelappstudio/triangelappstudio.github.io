Privacy Policy

How Triangel handles information across its app lineup.

This policy applies to Triangel applications including SnapPal, Resize Photo & Image, EchoVoice, iTranscriber, and related services published on the Apple App Store.

Introduction

Triangel ("us", "our", or "we") provides software applications on the Apple App Store. It is our policy to respect your privacy regarding any information we may collect while you use one of these applications.

Device Information

When you use our services on your mobile device, we may collect and record information such as the International Mobile Equipment Identity (IMEI), Identifier for Advertising (IDFA), Identifier for Vendor (IDFV), Integrated Circuit Card Identifier (ICCID), Media Access Control (MAC) address, device model, screen resolution, and language or region settings. We use this information to understand device usage, improve compatibility, and provide better support.

Personal Data

We do not collect personal data that identifies you directly, including names, home addresses, telephone numbers, dates of birth, or bank details. We also do not collect, record, or store face data.

Advertising

We may use device identifiers such as MAC address or IMEI for advertising measurement and tracking. This information is used to create a unique user number that helps count and evaluate advertising effectiveness. It is not sold to outside parties.

Third-Party Information Collection

Our services may include third-party tracking tools, APIs, or SDKs used by service providers on our behalf. These partners may access device identifiers, language region, approximate location context, or IP address for service delivery under their own privacy policies.

Third-party services that may be used include Firebase Crashlytics for tracking, prioritizing, and resolving app stability issues.

How We Use Information

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Communicate with users regarding updates or support
  • Analyze usage and improve app performance
  • Comply with legal obligations

Data Sharing

  • Service providers for storage, analytics, and operational support
  • Partners for marketing or promotions when applicable

Data Protection

  • Encryption during transmission
  • Secure storage solutions
  • Regular security assessment practices

User Rights

  • Accessing or updating your data when applicable
  • Requesting deletion of your data
  • Withdrawing consent where required by law

Children's Privacy

Our applications are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
